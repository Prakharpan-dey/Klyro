# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person on the other side is trying to help.

## What is expected

- Take the other person's point seriously before disagreeing with it, in code review as much as in conversation.
- Criticise the code, the idea or the decision — not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that people come in with different levels of experience, different first languages, and different amounts of time to give.
- When you are wrong, say so plainly and move on. Nobody keeps score here.

## What is not

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately wasting a contributor's time.

## Scope

This applies to everything in this project's spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and commits — and to any place where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately through the [Security tab](https://github.com/Prakharpan-dey/Klyro/security/advisories/new), which opens a private thread with the maintainers. Do not report it in a public issue, for the reporter's sake.

Reports are handled confidentially. Depending on what happened, the response ranges from a private word to an edited or removed comment, up to a block from the project. Whoever is handling a report stays out of it if they are personally involved.

This is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
